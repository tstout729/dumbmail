# Cloudbeds recipe

Checked against Cloudbeds' documentation on 2026-11-18; not yet run against a live account.

- **Webhooks.** Subscribed one action at a time with `POST /api/v1.3/postWebhook` (`object`, `action`, `endpointUrl`, optional `authHeaderName` and `authHeaderValue`). Deliveries are JSON POSTs; "order of the events is not guaranteed"; a failed delivery is retried after a minute, up to five attempts; `timestamp` is the original event time in Unix seconds. Ids are spelled `reservationID` in some events and `reservationId` in others, and the recipe reads both. Source: [Webhooks](https://developers.cloudbeds.com/docs/webhooks-1).
- **Authentication.** "Cloudbeds does not sign payloads": the supported way to authenticate deliveries is the header given as `authHeaderName`/`authHeaderValue` when subscribing. The recipe checks `X-Webhook-Token` (constant-time). There's no timestamp to check, so there's no signature window; a webhook only names a reservation, and what's recorded always comes from fetching it. Source: [Webhooks](https://developers.cloudbeds.com/docs/webhooks-1).
- **Events used.** `reservation/created`, `reservation/status_changed`, `reservation/dates_changed` (and the accommodation changes, if subscribed) fetch the reservation. `reservation/deleted` marks the reservation object deleted and not paid as of the event's `timestamp`, because a deleted reservation can't be fetched. Other events (`notes_changed`, guest events) are ignored. Source: [Webhooks](https://developers.cloudbeds.com/docs/webhooks-1).
- **The reservation.** `GET /api/v1.3/getReservation?reservationID=…` with an `x-api-key` header (scope `read:reservation`): `status` (`confirmed`, `not_confirmed`, `canceled`, `checked_in`, `checked_out`, `no_show`), `guestEmail` (the main guest), `guestList` (entries with `isMainGuest`, names and phones), `startDate`, `endDate`, `total`, `balance`, `balanceDetailed` (`grandTotal`, `paid`), `dateCreated`, `dateModified`, `dateCancelledUTC`, `isAnonymized`, `assigned`/`unassigned` (adults, children). Source: [getReservation](https://developers.cloudbeds.com/reference/get_getreservation-2).
- **Paid in full.** Cloudbeds has no "paid" flag. getReservation "includes balance (pending payment) and paid (amount already paid)". Paid in full means: a booked status, `total` above zero, `balance` exactly zero, `balanceDetailed.paid` at least the total, and `balanceDetailed.grandTotal` (when given) equal to the total. Missing or contradictory values mean not paid. Sources: [getReservation](https://developers.cloudbeds.com/reference/get_getreservation-2), [Reservation FAQs](https://developers.cloudbeds.com/docs/reservation-faqs).
- **Payments don't send webhooks.** There's no payment webhook for reservations, and getReservations warns that "some reservations modification may not be reflected" in `dateModified`. So every hour the pull lists confirmed arrivals (`GET /api/v1.3/getReservations?status=confirmed&checkInFrom=…&checkInTo=…`, 100 a page, the documented maximum) from yesterday to `payment_check_days` ahead, and fetches each one again. Source: [getReservations](https://developers.cloudbeds.com/reference/get_getreservations-2).
- **Versions.** Each fetched reservation is written to its `reservation` object (external id `cloudbeds:<id>`) with `as_of` set to when DumbMail received the answer (`input.response.received_at`), and `paid_checked_at` the same. A later answer is always received later, so an older answer (a retry, a slow run) never overwrites a newer one. The pre-arrival flow wants the evidence at least as new as the object's version and at most three hours old.
- **Events.** Reservation Booked (once, when first seen confirmed, checked in or checked out; its value is the total only when `settings.currency` is set, because getReservation doesn't say which currency it's in), Reservation Confirmed (a version per `dateModified`, status and dates, while confirmed or checked in), Reservation Cancelled (once, dated by `dateCancelledUTC`), Checked Out (once). A guest whose data was anonymized gets no profile and no events.
- **Consent.** Cloudbeds has no marketing opt-in field. Guests are held back from marketing; relay addresses (Booking.com, Expedia and others) are suppressed by the framework.

## Removed before anything is stored

The recipe keeps only what it reads (an allow-list): from webhooks the event, ids, status and timestamp; from getReservation the id, property, status, main guest's email, names and phones, dates, money totals and room occupancy counts. Guest documents, birthdates, tax ids, addresses, cards on file, custom fields (which may hold door or gate codes), notes and guest requirements are dropped.

## What a live check should look at first

- The exact format of `dateModified`, `dateCreated` and `dateCancelledUTC` (ISO 8601 or `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`, and in which zone).
- Whether `balance` and `balanceDetailed.paid` are numbers (not strings), and that a fully paid reservation shows `balance: 0` and `paid` equal to `total`.
- Whether `checkInFrom`/`checkInTo` take plain dates as documented, and that `pageSize=100` is accepted.
- That a `postWebhook` subscription with `authHeaderName`/`authHeaderValue` really sends the header on deliveries.
- Whether getReservation needs `propertyID` for keys that cover several properties.
