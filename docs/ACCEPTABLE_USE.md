# Acceptable use

Every DumbMail customer on the hosted service sends through the same email and texting accounts. When one sender mails people who never asked, everyone's email lands in spam. So these rules apply to everything you send through DumbMail.

## Send only to people who asked

- Email and text only people who signed up to hear from you: on your website, at checkout, in your shop, or in another way where they said yes to you.
- Don't import or send to bought, rented, scraped or shared lists, or to people whose details you collected for another reason.
- Don't send cold outreach: one-to-one style sales pitches to people who never signed up ("I came across your company", "quick question", "book a call with me"), or prospecting sequences.

## Be honest

- Don't pretend to be another company or person.
- Don't ask people for passwords, payment details or sign-in codes, and don't use links whose text hides where they go.
- Don't use deceptive subject lines or fake replies ("Re:" or "Fwd:" on a first message), fake urgency about someone's account, or a hidden sender.

## Not allowed at all

Anything illegal, and these categories, which our email and texting providers refuse:

- adult content
- gambling without a licence
- payday loans, debt relief and credit repair
- crypto investment schemes
- get-rich-quick schemes and multi-level marketing
- unlicensed pharmacy or prescription drugs
- weapons
- tobacco, vaping and cannabis promotion
- hate or harassment

## What happens

DumbMail checks new accounts, the files you import, the domains you add and what you send, mostly automatically (see [Sender screening](SCREENING.md)). Something unclear goes to a person. An account that breaks these rules can't send; it can still see its billing, export its data and close.
